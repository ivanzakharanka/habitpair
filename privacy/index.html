Habitpair Privacy Policy

  • No account, no trackingNo sign-in, no ads, no analytics.
  • Yours to export or eraseBoth are in Settings, any time.

Last updated November 6, 2026

What Habitpair stores

The habits you create (name, type and frequency) and the days you mark. This data is saved only on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. Habitpair has no account and no server, and it doesn’t use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. We can’t see your habits.

Backups

Your iPhone’s own backups, in iCloud or on a computer, can include Habitpair’s data under Apple’s terms. Deleting the app deletes its data from the device.

Exporting and erasing

Settings › Export Data creates a JSON file and you choose where it goes. Settings › Erase All Data permanently removes everything.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date above changes.

This page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log your IP address when you open it, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The Habitpair app itself sends nothing.
